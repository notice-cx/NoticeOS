# Home

Home answers one question: what changed since you last looked, and what needs you.

## What you see

**The greeting.** The page opens with **Good morning** (or afternoon, or evening) and a line such as **since 6 PM yesterday · 3 things changed**. Beside it, up to three small figures: **Yesterday** (revenue, marked **est.**), the month's pace, and yesterday's visitors. A figure that cannot be read yet is left out rather than shown as zero.

**The brief.** Up to five cards, the first drawn larger as the big thing. Each is one thing that changed: an alert, money, people, something shipped, a win or a milestone. A card wears its kind's tint and carries one short sentence, one shape (a line, a line over its usual band, or a figure) and at most one action, such as **Look**, **Money** or **Open**. Cards are ranked by severity, then dollars at stake.

The list ends with **That's everything since 6 PM yesterday.** and how old the reading is; with nothing to report it reads **Nothing changed since yesterday.**

**Decide.** At most three tasks waiting on you, each with its verb on the row: **Approve** for a gate, **Answer** or **Dismiss** for a question. A row's caption names the project and, for a gate, **needs your approval**. **All tasks** opens the board; **2 more** says what is left.

**Sites.** Every site in order with one health word. Only the two problem words carry a colour. **All sites →** opens the Sites page. With one site, Home shows that site's own numbers and chart under the brief instead.

**Before any site exists**, Home shows three steps: **Add your first site**, **Connect a source** and **See your first number**. Each step becomes its button when it is the current one, and reads as done afterwards. Home also offers your device's time zone as **Use \<zone>** in one press.

Home never describes the system unless something is broken, and then the broken thing arrives as a card.

## What you can do

- **Approve**, **Answer** or **Dismiss** act on a decision from its row.
- **Look**, **Money** or **Open** on a card opens the thing behind it.
- **All tasks** opens the Tasks board; **All sites →** opens the Sites page.
- **Add your first site**, on the first run, opens the **Add a site** dialog.
- **Use \<zone>** saves your device's time zone for every daily figure.

## States and labels

- The health words are **Setting up**, **On track**, **Monitor only**, **At risk** and **Off track**. Only **At risk** and **Off track** carry a colour.
- **est.** marks yesterday's revenue as an estimate until it is reconciled.
- A missing figure is left out or named, never drawn as zero. **not read yet** means nobody has measured it this session.
- A dash in a table is the trigger of its own tooltip, which says why.
- **Nothing changed since yesterday.** means the brief has nothing to report, not that nothing is watched.
