One mark. A disciplined system.

An N cut by a square notch: one solid shape, drawn in the ink of the surface it sits on and never recolored.

Keep the proportions fixed. Leave at least one quarter of the icon’s width clear around a lockup. Use blue for identity and interaction; operational states keep their own colors.

02 / Color

Quiet foundations.
A clear point of focus.

Ink and paper do most of the work. Notice blue identifies the product and guides actions. Click a swatch to copy its current theme value.

State has a separate language

✓ SucceededRecorded success
× FailedAction needed
! AttentionA known caveat
− PausedIntentionally inactive

Every state has a label and a distinct symbol. Blue never means that a workflow succeeded, and gray never pretends that missing evidence is healthy.

Data is a different kind of color

━ Revenue┄ Expenses

Cyan and violet identify business series. Labels and line patterns keep comparisons readable without color. Red and green remain available for actual outcomes and failures.

◉ RunningBlue also marks active execution.
