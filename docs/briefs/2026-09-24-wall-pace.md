# The Wall's today-vs-last-week pace — bead `ro-trai.43`

## The problem

On 2026-09-24 at 07:25 on the OS clock the Wall read "↓48%" in red on its first
site while both lines sat on the chart's floor. GA4's hourly series held hours
0–2 only — [hour, today, last week] = [0, 30, 50], [1, 22, 50], [2, 5, 44] —
so the pace compared hours 0–1: 52 users against 100. The arithmetic was right
and the verdict meaningless: two night hours, a sample nobody would bet on,
drawn as the day's slump on the money row at breakfast. The hours were already
on the OS clock (ingest re-buckets the property's own zone); the gap was
Google's processing lag, not a clock bug.

## Prior art

| Product | Source | What it does | Adopted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plausible — compare periods | <https://plausible.io/docs/compare-stats> | "If you use 'previous period', it will compare today's data only up to the current hour with the same time range from the previous day"; "Match day of week — aligns the comparison by day of the week". The chart draws both ranges as lines; the metric cards show the percent change against the comparison period. | Today is compared with the same weekday a week ago over the SAME hours only; both lines drawn; the window the percent covers is stated. |
| Google Analytics 4 — data freshness | <https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11198161> | Standard properties: "Standard intraday: 2-6 hours" of processing latency for reports and API queries. | The hours that count are the hours WITH PROCESSED DATA, never clock hours; the lag is drawn as the gap between where today's line ends and the now point at the clock. |
| NoticeOS's own flag rules | [docs/02-signal-contract.md](../02-signal-contract.md), `config/constants.json` `min_baseline_per_day` | A flag needs a baseline large enough to carry a verdict (volume-aware rules), never a verdict on a handful of events. | The pace states no percent below a floor of last week's users in the compared hours. |

## What was built

- **Silent until it can carry a verdict.** `intradayUsersPace` returns
  `paceChange: null` until the compared window has at least 4 completed hours
  with data AND at least 100 of last week's users in them
  (`PACE_VERDICT_MINIMUM`, one place, generic defaults). At 100 users the
  percent's ordinary noise is about ±10%, so a red reading (past −40%) is four
  times the noise. Every surface follows: no percent, no tone.
- **The window, named.** "↓10%" stands over "to 6 AM" on the Wall and in the
  site table; while the pace is silent, the Wall shows "to 2 AM" alone, muted —
  how far the provider's data reaches.
- **Now at the clock.** The breathing now point sits at the wall clock on the
  operator's zone. While the newest hour is the clock's, it stays on today's
  line as before; when the data lags, today's line ends in a small still dot
  and the now point waits on the floor at the clock.
