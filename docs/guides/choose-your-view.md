# Find a report

| What you want to know | Where to look |
|---|---|
| What needs attention today? | **Home** shows portfolio totals, open problems and decisions. Open a result for its details. |
| How are people finding and using an asset? | Open the asset in **Portfolio**. **Growth** shows traffic, **Search** shows search performance and **Journeys** shows the steps people complete. |
| What did we earn and spend? | Open **Money** for the portfolio or an asset’s **Money** tab for that asset. |
| What shipped and what is waiting? | **Tasks** lists work and decisions. **Agents** shows who delivered it. **Workflows** records automated work. |
| Is collection working? | **System health** shows collection and background operations. An asset’s **Health** tab shows its readings. **Alerts** lists problems that need attention. |

To add a source, open **Integrations**. To choose which property supplies an asset’s reports, open the asset’s **Data sources** tab.
