# Choose your view

A view changes the information each Tower page presents first. It is one personal choice shared across the Tower: **Overview**, **Growth**, **Revenue**, **Delivery** or **Reliability**. The same choices appear in normal workspaces and the public demo.

| View | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Performance, work and decisions |
| **Growth** | Opportunities to reach and keep visitors |
| **Revenue** | Earnings, costs and financial opportunities |
| **Delivery** | Work, progress and results |
| **Reliability** | Health, failures and recovery |

## Choose a view

The **View** bar appears below the header on every Tower page. Select a purpose to update the current page immediately. On a phone, open **View: \<current view>** and choose from the picker. Your current route, asset, tab, filters and open detail remain in place.

Home's first-run **What do you want to focus on?** offers the same five choices. One selection saves your personal preference. **Skip** closes the prompt without saving a choice. Choosing a personal view does not change the workspace default.

You can also select a view in **Settings** → **General** → **Your view**, or through Search (**⌘K**, **Ctrl+K**) under **Views**. A hosted workspace saves your choice per person across signed-in devices. Standalone and demo choices are saved in the current browser.

## What changes

Home changes its modules and their order. Other pages change their emphasis, summary context and supporting details for the selected purpose, including task and workflow details, asset pages, Settings and Integrations. Every route remains available with the same navigation and asset tabs. A view does not hide features, require a skill level, or switch between simple and technical vocabulary. Collection, measurements and other people's preferences stay the same.

Previously saved **At the top of Home** picks still promote matching Home modules. They are optional advanced preferences in Settings, separate from the single view choice.

## Workspace default and older choices

**Settings** → **General** → **Workspace default · for new people** sets the initial view for people who have not chosen one. With no saved default, the Tower starts in **Overview**. Changing it is an explicit workspace setting; onboarding never saves it automatically.

Older **I run the business**, **I build the product** and **Everything** choices open as **Overview**. Existing personal Home picks are preserved. A one-time notice directs people with an older business or product choice to select a new purpose from the bar or Settings.

## The Wall is separate

The [TV dashboard](/tower/wall) and its editor use a separate saved layout. Personal choices, workspace defaults and `?view=` parameters do not change it, including in the demo. With no saved layout, the Wall uses its default composition. Its editor offers independent **Health and decisions**, **Revenue and visitors** and **Portfolio overview** starting layouts.
