# Sites

A **site** is one website or product you added to NoticeOS. The **Sites** page compares them all side by side, and each site's name opens its own page, where everything about that one site lives.

Sites answers one question: which site needs me.

## What you see

**The Sites page** opens with its answer: **2 of 6 sites need you**, naming them, or the count of sites that are fine, beside the month's pace and yesterday's visitors against the same day a week before. **Add a site** sits in the header. Under the answer, one bar splits every site by its health word, worst first: **Off track** in red, **At risk** in amber, the calm words in grey. Then the sites compared, one row per site:

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Site** | its name, and under it the one health word: **Setting up**, **On track**, **Monitor only**, **At risk**, **Off track**, coloured only when it is a problem |
| **People a day** | the last four weeks as a line, and the latest finished day's count |
| **Yesterday** | yesterday's revenue and its change on the same day a week before |
| **Share of month** | the site's part of the month's revenue so far |
| **Sources** | one mark per data source, lit by its state, and how many are live of the ones the site uses |

A site's tasks, search clicks and ledger are on its own tabs. The columns drop as the window narrows, the name last: on a phone each row is the site, its health word and yesterday's money.

**A site's page** has a header with the site's name, its domain as a link, and its verdict: the same health word, with a hover that counts its open alerts. Under the header are the tabs: **Overview**, **Growth**, **Money**, **Search**, **Alerts**, **Tasks**, **Activity**, **Data sources** and **Settings**. The tab is in the address, so a link lands on the right one.

**Overview** answers on the first screen. A row of figures for money, people, search and the site's one product figure, each with its window, its change against the days before, its recent days as bars with their dates, and one line of context. Press a figure to draw it on the chart: bars with the days before as a dashed level, the best and lowest day named, every shipped change a labelled mark, and shaded spans for an open alert (with the rule's normal range), an outcome check or days not reported. Then **Data sources · last 30 days**, one row per source and one square per day, led by the source that failed most recently or missed the most days, and **Open alerts** for anything the chart does not already show. Under them: **Needs you**, **What matters**, **Bets** (the site's outcome checks, open ones first with the days left to their verdict), **Search movers**, **Product use** and the site's totals. A provider's name appears only as a chart key beside its own line.

**The other tabs:**

- **Growth**: what moved against the days before, one row per series, above the **Audience** and search charts; then the tracked search terms and product use.
- **Money**: the month as one sum, revenue minus cost equals net. Beside it, the month's ad revenue days against a usual day and the month so far against last month, both marked as estimates. Then daily revenue over the chosen range and the site's ledger, as on the Money page.
- **Search**: **Queries that moved**, each drawn where it sits on the results page now against before; the tracked terms, each with its rank over time; then **Search context**: links, AI mentions, competitors and opportunities.
- **Alerts**: the last 30 days as a timeline, one row per condition, then **Open** with each reading against its normal range, **Snoozed**, **No longer current** and **History**.
- **Tasks**: the site's slice of the Tasks board.
- **Activity**: the last 90 days of changes and bets on one timeline, a bet marked where it was registered and again where its verdict came in. Under it, the **Timeline** of recorded changes and the bets still being watched.
- **Data sources**: how the site's data arrives, drawn as one picture with a failing source lit where it sits; then one row per source with **Connect**, **Not using** and the mapping fields, and **More sources** for the optional ones.
- **Settings**: one column of cards: **Identity**, **Manual lifecycle stage**, **Automation**, **Data collection**, **Card totals**, **Alert rules in force**, **Tracked search terms** and **Archive**.

## What you can do

- **Add a site** opens the same dialog Home uses.
- The health bar's legend is the **Health** filter: press **2 at risk** to show only those sites, press it again to show them all.
- **Order** puts the sites in **Your order** (the saved order Home and the TV use), **Needs you** first, by **Money** this month, by **People** or **A–Z**. The stage list narrows to one lifecycle stage. On a phone both wait behind **Order & stage**.
- A narrowed list says how many it shows and offers **Clear**. Every choice is in the address, so a link keeps it.
- A site's name opens its page; the domain link on that page opens the site itself.
- Press a figure on the Overview to draw it on the chart; arrow keys step through its days.
- Hover, focus or tap a mark on the Overview chart to read the change or outcome check behind it.
- On **Activity**, **Log a change** opens one form with two choices: **Something happened** records a change, and **Watch an outcome** registers an outcome check against one.
- On **Data sources**, **Connect** opens the connect panel and **Not using** records a reason for skipping a source.
- On **Settings**, the cards hold everything that applies to this one site.

## States and labels

- A site that reported nothing says so in its row: **no visitors reported**, **no revenue**, never a zero.
- With no sites the page reads **No sites yet**; with one, it is that site's row.
- A site with no numbers yet opens on **Data sources** instead of an empty Overview.
- A **hollow point** is a day still being counted; it fills in once the day is complete.
- A **dashed line** is a projection or last week's comparison, never a measurement.
- A **bracketed day** on the axis is one the measurement cannot compare, such as a day a reporting time zone changed; its colour verdict is withdrawn.
- A **dash** in a figure is a named absence. Hover it for the reason.
- **unknown** means the data cannot say, which is different from a measured zero, which is drawn.
