# `domain-costs.json` — what each asset's names actually cost

Paid domain orders, recorded when names are bought or renewed, from the
registrar's receipts or export; the saved orders give the ledger a durable
record of those costs.

**Seed and export, not source of truth** (D22): `pnpm config:seed` loads this
installation's copy (else this generic default) into the store's
`config_documents` table, the running OS reads and saves it there, and
`pnpm config:export` writes it to `installation/`. Until an install seeds,
every read falls back to the copy compiled into the Workers.

### What the Tower may edit here

Generated by `pnpm config:docs -- --write` from `scripts/config-registers.mts`, the one declaration of every register and knob; `scripts/config-docs.test.mjs` fails when this block is stale.

**Domain orders** (`domain-costs`) — one prepaid domain order. Rows live at `/domains` of `config/domain-costs.json` (array, keyed by `domain`); edited from /financials.

| Field | Label | Type | Required | Hint | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `domain` | Domain | `string` | yes | lowercase, a name not a URL: example.com | up to 253 characters; matches `^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9.-]*\.[a-z]{2,}$`; fixed once written |
| `asset` | Site | `asset-id` | yes | the site that carries the cost | — |
| `kind` | Order | `enum` | yes | what was bought | one of `registration`, `renewal`, `transfer` |
| `paidUsd` | Paid (USD) | `number` | yes | as charged on the receipt | at least 0 |
| `paidOn` | Paid on | `date` | yes | the receipt date | — |

## How it books

A domain is a **prepaid annual term**, so each order amortizes evenly over 12
months from its purchase month and books as an `infra` cost on its asset
(`scripts/cost-import.mjs`). Cash-basis was rejected: a premium name would make
one month look catastrophic and eleven look free, and monthly margin is the
number this ledger exists to make legible. All of an asset's domains sum into
**one** `infra` row per month, under source `domains`, so the row stays one
figure per asset per month and idempotent.

## Attribution

Only names that belong to a site in the portfolio are here; a registrar's
export covers every domain the operator holds, and booking another venture's
registrations would overstate every site's cost. Each site's own domain is
unambiguous; defensive and variant registrations are a judgement, grouped by
the site they protect, and the holding entity's own names go to the OS's own
asset as portfolio overhead. The `asset` field is one edit away if wrong.

## The caveat that matters

**A registration price is not a renewal price.** A first-year promotion or
premium name can make the initial charge differ from later renewals. The
ledger records what was actually paid; add renewal orders from their receipts
rather than changing previous orders, because a booked month is history.

## Where it is edited

**`/financials` → *Declared costs***, the same section that edits
[`recurring-costs.json`](recurring-costs.README.md): an order is added,
corrected or removed through the validated write lane, one changeset per
action, with Undo and the month totals recomputed after the save. The
amortization facts a row cannot carry — what has been paid in total, how many
orders are still spreading into the shown month, each asset's declared run
rate — are summarised above the rows. Outside the Tower, `pnpm config:apply`
makes the same store write.
